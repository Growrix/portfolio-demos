// BUSINESS IMPACT

Before vs After

✗ BEFORE — MANUAL TRIAGE
  • Support inbox fills up — no systematic process
  • Someone reads each email manually to decide priority
  • Urgent complaints sit unread until someone checks
  • No shared log — team has no visibility
  • Draft replies written from scratch every time
  • Escalations take hours or days
  • No data on support volume or trends
✓ AFTER — AI AUTO-TRIAGE
  • Every new email classified automatically — Gmail checked about every 2 minutes
  • AI sets category (Billing / Account / Damage / Inquiry / Spam) and priority (urgent / normal)
  • Urgent or damage emails trigger a Slack escalation
  • Every email logged to a shared Google Sheet as "Pending Review"
  • Draft reply ready for review — the team checks and sends
  • ~30s from trigger to Slack · about 2–3 min worst case from email arrival
  • Error Monitor Zap posts to Slack if a run fails

How Each Step Is Built

1
Trigger — Gmail · New Email Matching Search
POLLING

New Email Matching Search — not "New Email" (avoids picking up all mail)

in:inbox -from:me -label:ai-triaged -category:promotions -category:social -category:updates

Polling — Gmail checked about every 2 minutes

From, Subject, Body — used by the next steps

✓ Only new inbox mail · Skips own mail, promotions, social, updates and already-triaged emails
2
Action 1 — Formatter by Zapier · Text
CLEAN-UP

Text transform

Gmail Subject (dynamic from Step 1)

Tidies the subject line before AI and logging

On every email

✓ Clean, consistent subject in the AI prompt and the Sheets log
3
Action 2 — AI by Zapier · Classify Support Email
AI STEP

From + Subject + Body (dynamic from earlier steps)

Return JSON · Mark urgent if the customer is angry or the issue is critical · Mark Spam if promotional

Billing · Account · Damage · Inquiry · Spam

urgent · normal

✓ Structured output · category, priority, reasoning, draft_reply (draft is for human review only)
4
Action 3 — Google Sheets · Create Spreadsheet Row
ALWAYS RUNS

Support triage log — client's Google account

Email details · Category · Priority · AI draft reply · Status

"Pending Review" (team updates it after replying)

Runs before the filter, so nothing is lost
