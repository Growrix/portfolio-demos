WF1Capture → Score → ReplyLIVE

Validates and de-duplicates every submission, has AI return a structured JSON score, runs guardrail checks, writes the CRM row and sends a personalised Gmail reply with a Cal.com booking link. Optional Slack alert for hot leads.

Form→Dedupe→AI JSON→Guardrails→Sheets→Gmail
WF2Follow-up SchedulerLIVE

Runs hourly. Sends up to 3 follow-ups in the same Gmail thread, respects quiet hours, skips leads that booked or opted out, and closes the lead as no-response after step 3.

Hourly→Due leads→Quiet hrs · booked · opt-out→Reply in thread
WF3Booking SyncLIVE

Receives the Cal.com booking webhook, verifies its signature, ignores duplicate deliveries (idempotency), marks the lead Booked in the CRM and stops any further follow-ups.

Cal.com webhook→Verify signature→Idempotency→Mark Booked
WF4Error HandlerLIVE

Catches failures from the other workflows and emails an alert. Rate-limited to 1 alert per 15 minutes so an outage can't flood the inbox.

Error trigger→Rate limit 1 / 15 min→Alert email

// SAFETY BY DESIGN

AI that talks to customers, with guardrails

An automated reply goes out under the business's name, so every AI output is checked before anything is sent.

💲 No prices

Replies can't quote prices or estimates. Pricing stays a human conversation.

🚫 No guarantees

Promises like "guaranteed same-day" or "fixed for good" are not allowed through.

🔗 Booking link only

The only link allowed in a reply is the booking link. Extra links fail the check.

🛡 Prompt-injection flag

Form text that tries to give the AI orders ("ignore previous instructions…") is flagged and gets no AI reply.

⚠️ Safety flag

Hazards such as a gas line raise a safety flag on the lead so a person sees it.

🧾 Structured JSON

The AI returns a structured JSON score, so routing decisions come from fields rather than free text.

// BUSINESS IMPACT

Before vs After

✗ BEFORE · MANUAL LEAD HANDLING
  • Web enquiries wait until someone is off the tools to check email
  • Urgent jobs (burst pipes, leaks) look the same as routine quotes
  • Spam mixed in with real customers
  • Leads tracked in heads, notebooks or scattered inboxes
  • Booking means phone tag back and forth
  • Follow-ups forgotten when the week gets busy
  • Nobody notices if the automation quietly breaks
✓ AFTER · AI SPEED-TO-LEAD ENGINE
  • Personalised reply in under 10 s in the demo runs, day or night
  • AI scores every lead hot / warm / cold / spam
  • Spam and prompt-injection attempts get no reply
  • Every lead logged to a shared Google Sheets CRM
  • Booking link in the first email · bookings sync back automatically
  • 3-step follow-up in the same thread, stops once they book
  • Error alerts by email, rate-limited so they stay useful
