Test set: 20 briefs, 20 handled correctly

The test set mixes normal briefs with thin, duplicate, oversized, prompt-injection and risky-claim briefs. These are demo test results from two consecutive runs of the evaluation workflow; the saved run replayed here is one of them.

How it works

1 Brief formPublic form with a daily cap and duplicate detection.
2 AI draftAn AI model writes title, meta description and sections.
3 QA15 rule checks: length, keyword use, headings, banned claims, planted instructions.
4 ReviewSlack card, then a confirm page; a link click alone changes nothing.
5 ExportApproved draft goes to a Google Doc and an email; every step is logged.

Scope and limits

  • No website publishing and no search-console connection in this build — drafts stay in Google Docs.
  • The AI can be wrong; the checks reduce risk, the human approval is the final gate.
  • Risky health or money claims are removed or flagged before review.
  • Scheduled parts (for example a review reminder) are part of the design and are switched off in this demo.
  • Built with n8n, a Postgres database, Slack, Google Docs and Google Sheets.
