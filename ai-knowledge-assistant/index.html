// GUARDRAILS

Safe by design

Answers only from documents

No sources, no answer — it says so and points to the office.

Real citations

Page numbers come from stored metadata, never from the AI’s memory.

Injection-proof

Text in a document can’t give the assistant orders.

Newest policy wins

Conflicts are shown and the newer document applies.

Abuse limits

10 questions per session per day, 500 characters max.

Measured

A 30-question eval re-runs after every change.
