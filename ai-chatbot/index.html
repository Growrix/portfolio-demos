WF3Escalate to Human (tool)LIVE

Creates a handoff ticket, pauses the bot for that chat, posts the last 10 messages to Slack #support-escalations and stores the Slack message id.

Handoff row→Transcript→Slack
WF4Knowledge-Base LoadREADY

Loads 5 company documents (prices, service area, policies, FAQ, company) → 500-char chunks → OpenAI embeddings → pgvector. Re-run to update what the bot knows — no workflow change.

Docs→Chunk + embed→pgvector

// GUARDRAILS

AI that talks to customers, safely

Grounded answers

Must search the knowledge base before answering; prices are always “starting at”, confirmed on site.

No invented facts

Unknown price, time slot or technician name → honest “I don’t have that” + callback offer.

Safety first

Gas smell → leave the building, call 911/utility from outside, then escalate. Flooding → shut the main valve.

Server-side validation

The AI can’t write bad data: ZIP, phone/email and every category are re-checked in the workflow.

No data exposure

The agent has no “read leads” tool — nothing to leak. Prompt-injection attempts get a standard refusal.

Cost caps

30 messages per chat, 1,000 characters per message, 200 new chats per day — enforced in SQL before the AI runs.
