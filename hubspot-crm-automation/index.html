// GUARDRAILS

Safe by design

No duplicates

Upsert by email; the same lead never gets two deals or two sequences.

Instant unsubscribe

One click or one reply stops everything and updates HubSpot.

Preview-proof links

Link scanners can’t click for the lead; only a confirmed button press counts.

Injection-proof

Text in the form can’t change the score rules.

Email allow-list

Demo emails go only to owner test inboxes.

Measured

25-case eval re-runs after every change.
